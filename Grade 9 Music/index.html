Key Signatures & Order of Accidentals

Master the secrets high school choir singers use to sight-read any key signature in seconds.

Staff:
The Sharp Rule (♯) Order: F - C - G - D - A - E - B

Mnemonic: “Father Charles Goes Down And Ends Battle”

Choir Cheat Code:
1. Find the last sharp on the far right. That note is Ti (the leading tone).
2. Go up 1 half step to find Do (Your Major Key!).
Example: 1 sharp (F♯) ➔ half step up is G Major. 2 sharps (F♯, C♯) ➔ half step up from C♯ is D Major.
The Flat Rule (♭) Order: B - E - A - D - G - C - F

Mnemonic: “Battle Ends And Down Goes Charles’ Father” (Spells BEAD + GCF)

Choir Cheat Code:
1. Look at the second-to-last flat on the right.
2. That flat is the Major Key!
Example: 2 flats (B♭, E♭) ➔ 2nd to last is B♭ Major. 3 flats (B♭, E♭, A♭) ➔ E♭ Major.
★ Only exception to memorize: 1 flat (B♭) is F Major.

Scales & The Choir Solfège Ladder

Interactive Solfège syllables (Do-Re-Mi-Fa-Sol-La-Ti-Do) and the whole/half step pattern formulas.

Clef:
Major Scale Step Formula W - W - H - W - W - W - H

Whole step = 2 piano keys. Half step = 1 piano key. Notice the half steps (H) are always between Mi - Fa (3rd-4th) and Ti - Do (7th-8th)!

Natural Minor Step Formula W - H - W - W - H - W - W

Minor scales have a darker, sadder mood. The half steps fall between La - Ti and Do - Re (2nd-3rd and 5th-6th).

High School SATB Choir Vocal Ranges

Which voice part does Connor sing in choir? Find your clef and comfortable range.

Soprano (S) Treble 𝄞
High female or treble voice. Sits near the top of the treble clef.
Range: C4 (Middle C) to A5
Alto (A) Treble 𝄞
Lower female or unchanged treble voice. Reads the bottom of treble staff.
Range: F3 to D5
Tenor (T) 𝄢 / 𝄞 8vb
Higher male changing voice. Bridges the top of Bass and lower Treble.
Range: C3 to G4
Bass / Baritone (B) Bass 𝄢
Lower male voice. Reads the lower and middle regions of the bass clef.
Range: E2 to C4 (Middle C)
